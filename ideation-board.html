1. Student Friction & Guardian Mindmap

Root Cause & System Architecture

The Root Problem

University Burnout & Task Paralysis

Students stall not because they lack motivation, but because accumulated pressure creates an undivided wall of fear with no clear starting point.

1. Why stall? Work has no visible finish line.
2. Why no step? The week feels like an undivided wall.
3. Why a wall? No system explains pressure sources.
4. Why fail? Tools track time but ignore capacity & anxiety.
5. The Fix: Humanized guardians, consent pacing & zero guilt.

Kai

Clock Tower · Time
Friction: Time Blindness Thursday reads 103% overload, leading to panic, freeze response, and complete avoidance.
Solution: Consent Pacing Explainable Daily Load + dashed previews to safely move tasks only with explicit student approval.

Mira

Library · Cognitive
Friction: Blank Page Dread Assignments feel too vast and ambiguous to begin; fear of doing it wrong stalls initial effort.
Solution: 1 Checkpoint Shrinks big assignments into 1 concrete finish line with an explicit, student-owned Definition of Done.

Sky

Café · Social & Mood
Friction: Academic Shame Students feel isolated, falling behind peers, and dreading judgment or productivity scorecards.
Solution: Warm Presence Co-presence without pressure; soothing tea ritual with zero wrong moves and unconditional support.

Sol

Garden & Pond · Physical
Friction: Rest Guilt Taking breaks feels like wasted time, creating a spiral of exhaustion and constant guilt.
Solution: Unpenalized Rest Gentle Ripples pond with no timers, no win/lose states, and no streak or attendance pressure.

Goh

Friction: Life Admin Drag Chores and errands quietly consume mental bandwidth without getting acknowledged in study plans.
Solution: Boundary Framing Separates fixed life admin from deep study so schedules reflect realistic human capacity.

2. Market Research & Empirical Validation

Behavioral Evidence & Case Precedents
Academic Research Grounding

ScienceDirect Study on Gamified Task Management (2023)

"A study of 318 Malaysian users of gamified task-management apps found that usefulness, attitude, and habit were significant drivers of users’ intention to continue using the apps. The researchers’ model explained 55% of the variation in continuance intention."

Core Product Takeaway: Gamification isn’t just about making tasks fun — when it creates useful habits and restorative experiences, it gives overwhelmed students a sustainable reason to return without burning out.

Industry Case References & Value Distinction

1. Focus Friend (Idle Tracking Precedent)

Demonstrated that students respond enthusiastically to gamified focus when it rewards phone idle time rather than enforcing harsh device lockouts.

2. Desktop Pet Companions & Cozy MMORPGs

Validated that co-presence (having an avatar companion sit with you while working) measurably reduces solo academic isolation and anxiety.

3. The PaceTown Value Distinction

Unlike generic productivity tools (Notion, Google Calendar) that simply list tasks as an intimidating wall, PaceTown actively protects human capacity with explainable loads, consent rebalancing, and zero-guilt rest.

3. Breadth of Exploration — Concept Matrix

Alternative Paradigms Evaluated
Concept A · Dropped

The Strict Warden

Aggressive website blocker + streak tracker that locks devices and penalizes missed deadlines.

Why Dropped: Streaks and lockouts induce shame spirals and panic. Students find workarounds and associate the app with anxiety.
Concept B · Dropped

Study Tamagotchi

A cute digital pet that falls ill or dies if daily study sessions and tasks are skipped.

Why Dropped: Guilt-based mechanics exploit emotional manipulation. Unethical for overwhelmed students who need restoration, not guilt.
Concept C · Dropped

Cloud AI Ghostwriter

LLM assistant that generates assignment summaries, outlines, and paragraphs automatically.

Why Dropped: Destroys academic integrity, strips learning agency, and introduces dependency on unreliable cloud APIs.
Concept D · Chosen

PaceTown (Living Campus)

Consent-gated workload rebalancing in a cozy 2D pixel-art campus with local restorative pacing.

Why Chosen: Protects student autonomy, offers tangible starting momentum, can operates locally offline/optional online mode with AI, and eliminates productivity guilt.

Iteration 1 (V1) Pivoted

Red Alert Dashboard

A standard web table displaying commitments with flashing red percentage warning bars.

Pivot Rationale: User testing showed red alert numbers triggered acute heart-rate spikes. We replaced alerts with ambient "Load Weather" (soft mist/rain).
Iteration 2 (V2) Pivoted

Massive Open-World RPG

A sprawling multi-district RPG world requiring 30 seconds of walking between buildings.

Pivot Rationale: Long transit times aggravated student fatigue. We compressed the campus into a single cozy grove and built the instant-access Town List.
Iteration 3 (V3) Pivoted

Autonomous Auto-Scheduler

An AI algorithm that silently shifted overloaded Thursday tasks into open weekend slots.

Pivot Rationale: Moving items without permission caused disorienting loss of control. We invented the Consent Preview (soft dashed borders requiring approval).
Iteration 4 (Final) Shipped

Restorative Living Campus

16-frame synced walk cycles, local-first IndexedDB storage, accessible D-pads, and unpenalized recovery.

Final Vertical Slice: 378/378 unit tests passing, sub-30MB production bundle, and zero external cloud failure points.

5. Mentor Consultation Log

Real Feedback & Design Decisions
M1

Scope, Precedents & Value Distinction

Mentor Consultation Session · 4 Sept 2026
Mentor Guidance: "Gamifying workflow and stress management does not deviate from the challenge brief; it makes the project stand out compared to generic productivity dashboards. Ground it with established precedents (MMORPG mechanics, desktop companions, Focus Friend) and clearly define what makes the solution distinct and better than existing tools beyond just having a game aesthetic."
Concrete Changes Made:
  • Articulated our core value distinction: Consent-first rebalancing rather than passive task lists.
  • Connected game mechanics to psychological safety (Load Weather instead of red alerts; zero-guilt recovery).
  • Structured rich multi-layered process mapping illustrating the user journey from friction to restoration.
M2

Demo Presentation & Feature Bundling

Mentor Consultation Session · 11 Sept 2026
Mentor Guidance: "Rather than walking through every individual mechanic or screen, bundle features into 2–3 clear conceptual groups. Highlight purpose over mechanics: avoid getting bogged down in micro-details (like individual form inputs or step clicks). Clearly communicate what each feature is and why it matters to the user. Keep the pace engaging with short clips, and provide an interactive link so judges can explore firsthand."
Concrete Changes Made:
  • Streamlined the product narrative into 3 core pillars: Smart Time Management, Guided Productivity, and Stress Relief/Recovery.
  • Shifted all feature descriptions to student emotional benefit rather than technical form clicks.
  • Replaced long text walkthroughs with focused, bite-sized visual gameplay anchors.
  • Provided a direct one-click interactive launcher (index.html) for judges to explore.
