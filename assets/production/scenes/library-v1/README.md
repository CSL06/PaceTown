## Prompt

Create a warm, bright, sunlit university library interior for PaceTown as a polished, cozy pixel-art RPG background. Use an elevated near-top-down perspective and a landscape 3:2 composition. Place a wide entrance at the bottom, a friendly mentor consultation desk on the right, an individual student work desk in the upper center, and an enchanted reference book on a pedestal on the left. Keep the center and lower-middle floor open and navigable. Surround the room with tall bookshelves, arched windows, indoor plants, soft rugs, warm wood and cream stone. Use cheerful morning sunshine, honey wood, leafy green, teal, sky blue and muted gold. Background only: no people, text, labels, UI, logos, watermark, calendar, clocks, or presentation board.

## Runtime use

The interaction labels, Mira sprite, player sprite, and focus states are layered in HTML/CSS so they remain accessible and responsive.
