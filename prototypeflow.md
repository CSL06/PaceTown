# PaceTown — 75-Second Game Trailer Flow

Record this like a mobile-game commercial: **fast cuts, big transformations, short lines**. No feature tour. The viewer should feel the pressure, see the town react, and want to play.

## Before recording

```powershell
npm run dev
```

Use a fresh private window, or visit `/reset`, so the seeded week is restored. Capture at 1080p, keep the cursor obvious, and record the screen silently first. Add voice-over and punchy captions in editing.

## The cut

| Time | Screen action | Voice-over / caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Open on the live landing town. Hover the overloaded day, then hit **Start today's mission**. | **"Your week is at 103%. What do you do?"** |
| 0:06–0:13 | Flash through Town Hall: plain-language commitments → visible assumptions → Daily Load at 102.9%. | **"PaceTown turns the chaos into one honest picture."** |
| 0:13–0:22 | Enter Clock Tower. Show locked class/shift cards, then Kai's three move previews. Hit **Approve** and let Thursday drop. | **"Locked means locked. But the things that can move? You stay in control."**<br>**MAKE SPACE.** |
| 0:22–0:32 | Cut to Mira in the Library. Pick ERD assignment → "I don't know where to start" → reveal one small checkpoint. | **"Then turn the scary task into one step you can actually start."**<br>**ONE QUEST. 15 MINUTES.** |
| 0:32–0:40 | Show the study desk: Done when, timer, Ask Mira, a short answer, then record partial progress and the next action. | **"Get unstuck. Save your progress. A rough start still counts."** |
| 0:40–0:50 | Hard cut to Garden Pavilion. Start Gentle Ripples: hold/release three waves, leaves scatter, hold briefly on clear water, then the lotus blooms. | **"And when your brain needs air—take it. No streaks. No guilt. No failure state."**<br>**RESET. BREATHE. BLOOM.** |
| 0:50–0:57 | Cut to Pocket of Green. Select an outside or window option and self-confirm it. Briefly show the optional pixel-photo keepsake. | **"Go outside. Find green. Your word is enough."** |
| 0:57–1:07 | Show Journal timeline: rebalance → small work win → recovery. Pan across a brighter town / growing garden. | **"Make space. Finish what matters. Rest on purpose."** |
| 1:07–1:15 | Final calm town shot. Land on the start button or logo. | **"PaceTown. Find your pace. Grow your place."** |

## Recording beats

- Keep each screen clip **2–4 seconds**. Cut on button presses, map entries, approval, task selection, and the lotus bloom.
- Let the **Thursday load number falling** and the **lotus bloom** breathe for an extra second; those are the two payoff shots.
- Use impact captions sparingly: `103% CAPACITY`, `MAKE SPACE`, `ONE SMALL STEP`, `RESET. BREATHE. BLOOM.`, `NO STREAKS. NO SHAME.`
- Do not explain formulas, settings, parser mechanics, or every guardian in the trailer. Save those for the live demo or Q&A.

## One-breath pitch

> **Your week is overloaded. PaceTown shows what is weighing you down, helps you move what can move with your permission, turns work into one doable quest, and makes recovery part of progress—not a detour from it.**

## If a shot goes wrong

- Reset at `/reset` and retake only that clip.
- For Gentle Ripples, wait for the **clear-water pause** and the **lotus bloom**; do not cut while a wave is still travelling.
- If movement is awkward, use the Town List to jump to Clock Tower, Library, Garden Pavilion, or Park. The edit should feel instant anyway.
